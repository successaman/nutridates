<p><strong>Chocolate that works harder.</strong></p>
<p>NutriDates Cocoa Dates Milkshake Mix combines cocoa, dates, oats and protein
into a 30-second chocolate shake — built for students, professionals,
fitness-conscious drinkers and families who want real nutrition without
giving up taste.</p>

<h3>Why it works</h3>
<ul>
  <li>17g protein when made with milk (11g from the powder alone, per 32g serving)</li>
  <li>Dates and cocoa for genuine chocolate taste — not "diet" flavoured</li>
  <li>Ready in 30 seconds: scoop, pour milk, shake</li>
  <li>Fits breakfast, post-workout, study breaks or an evening chocolate craving</li>
</ul>

<h3>How to prepare</h3>
<p>Add 2 heaped scoops (about 32g) to roughly 200ml of cold or warm milk.
Shake or blend for 10 seconds and drink.</p>

<h3>Ingredients</h3>
<p>Soya Protein Isolate, Dates Powder, Oats Powder, Skimmed Milk Powder,
Cocoa, Dry Fruits Powder, Guar Gum, Soya Lecithin, Salt.</p>

<h3>Nutrition (per 100g / per 32g serving)</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Energy: 416 kcal / 133 kcal</li>
  <li>Protein: 34g / 11g</li>
  <li>Carbohydrate: 45g / 14.4g</li>
  <li>Natural sugar: 25g / 8g</li>
  <li>Dietary fibre: 6g / 1.9g</li>
  <li>Total fat: 7g / 2.2g</li>
</ul>

<h3>Allergen information</h3>
<p>Contains oats, nuts and soy. Manufactured in a facility that also
processes dairy and whey.</p>

<h3>Storage &amp; shelf life</h3>
<p>Store in an airtight container in a cool, dry place away from direct
sunlight. Best before 90 days from packaging.</p>
